### Reusable partner inquiry — prepared, not sent

Request one written capability/terms matrix for this precise flow, before any onboarding or purchase:

1. Can your approved Argentina/ARS arrangement collect the full agreed job price upfront, retain it outside Changeo's operating funds and release only under validated final-acceptance terms? Identify legal owners, eligible services/participants, guardian-paid jobs and local/remote/shipped coverage.
2. What executable review/release choices, maximum holding/dispute/inactivity limits and explicit-approval-only resolution paths are supported? Feedback stages release nothing. Explain limits around pending moderated communication.
3. Can you support original-source full/partial refunds before/after payout, failed payouts/refunds, chargebacks and unavailable provider balances? State fees, withholding, reserves, recovery liability and subsidy mechanism preserving customer charge/provider amount.
4. Can legal payers explicitly elect fully backed, nontransferable, no-paid-top-up, nonexpiring reusable refund credits, including guardians and mixed-source funding? Explain backing, closure/redemption, restrictions and legal treatment; any necessary default change requires a source-contract decision.
5. Supply KYC/account requirements, authenticated callbacks, idempotency/unknown-outcome lookup, balance/transaction reports, reconciliation duties and sandbox/test-identity availability. Quote all collection, custody, payout, refund and recurring-billing costs separately.

These are provider/professional evidence tasks, not five more founder decisions. Outreach remains subject to separate authority.

## Conditional zero-yield model

All amounts here are USD public-price arithmetic, before applicable taxes, FX/payment charges and add-ons. Input volumes are invented **sensitivity examples, not forecasts, approved budgets or capacity claims**. Reference app USD25 + development DB USD15 + email USD20 = USD60 fixed/month. Use Plus headline identity pricing conditionally: `max(99, 1.39 × checks)`. Vendor calculator/add-on ambiguity means this is not an invoice quote.

For text-only policy classification, assume 1,200 ordinary input + 200 output tokens per revision: `(1200 × 0.40 + 200 × 1.60) / 1,000,000 = USD0.0008/revision`. This excludes images, retries, tools and extraction. R2 storage examples stay within operation free allowances and use integer GB-month units after the 10 GB free amount.

Adding a same-size private staging app/development DB would add USD40/month as a planning example. **Excluded and still required:** production sizing/HA, additional workers/nodes, independent backups/restore environment, observability/security/file scanning/extraction, image/audio/media processing, retries, identity add-ons/guardian checks, payment/custody/payout/refund/subscription fees, reserves and fraud losses, legal/accounting/tax setup, support/manual appeals, rewards and **substantial marketing**. The technical subtotal is not the launch funding requirement.

Use a real treasury model once partner quotes and founder funding exist:

```text
monthly result ARS = paid subscriptions net of tax/refunds/free periods
                   - all job and billing fees/subsidies
                   - rewards, fraud and chargeback losses
                   - support, review, compliance and marketing
                   - USD vendor costs × actual all-in ARS/USD funding rate
break-even paid providers = fixed monthly costs / positive net contribution per paid provider
free-period funding need = setup + operating burn over approved free period
                         + own-funded reserves/rewards + acquisition tranches
```

Customer custody and refund-credit liabilities stay separately backed; they are neither revenue nor working capital. Fee calculations must demonstrate the exact agreed customer charge and provider service amount after validated tax treatment. Hypothetical fee sensitivity on an ARS100,000 job is ARS1,000/3,000/5,000 at 1%/3%/5%; these are assumptions, **not all-in partner rates**. At 1,000 such jobs, that subsidy alone is ARS1m/3m/5m. More transactions can worsen losses under flat subscriptions.

**Commercial draft to cost, not approve:** retain a free founding cohort followed by an explicitly disclosed trial/monthly plan; compare trial beginning at first approved provider activation against signup to avoid consuming it while onboarding is pending. Model several prices from willingness to pay and net cohort contribution before recommending an amount. Budget initial subscription credits/badges with caps and fraud adjustments; release later visibility/cash benefits only under the documented stages. Set own-funds subsidy/reserve limits before accepting new funding. Exact prices, durations, caps and thresholds still need approval, but the assistant can prepare those proposals from actual funding and quotes.

**Support/capacity draft:** budget by queue work, not account counts. For example, 1,000 reviewed revisions with a hypothetical 2% manual rate at five minutes each require 100 minutes; ten hypothetical disputes at 30 minutes add 300 minutes: 6 hours 40 minutes before identity, appeals, billing, reconciliation and interruptions. This is a planning sensitivity, not measured quality or an SLA. Derive sustainable volume and operating hours from actual staff availability, then calibrate manual rate, response/recovery and alert targets in authorized tests. Pause new risky activity when funded capacity is exhausted while preserving existing rights. Founder need not invent JVM limits or alert thresholds.
